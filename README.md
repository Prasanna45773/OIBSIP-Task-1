# OIBSIP-Task-1

📊 Retail Sales EDA

Exploratory Data Analysis of a retail sales dataset using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to identify sales trends, customer behaviour, and business insights.

🎯 Objectives
Inspect and clean the dataset
Perform descriptive statistical analysis
Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends
Analyze customer age groups and gender
Compare revenue and quantity by product category
Identify correlations between numerical variables
Generate actionable business recommendations

🛠️ Tech Stack
Python
Pandas
NumPy
Matplotlib
Seaborn
Jupyter Notebook

📈 Key Analysis
Monthly & Quarterly Sales Trends
Customer Demographics
Revenue by Product Category
Quantity Sold by Category
Correlation Heatmap
Revenue by Gender

💡 Key Insights
May 2023 recorded the highest monthly sales.
Q4 2023 had the highest sales among complete quarters.
Electronics generated the highest revenue.
Clothing had the highest quantity sold.
Price per Unit had a strong positive correlation with Total Amount.
